## Long description
239 item icons for RPGs, roguelikes, crafting and farming games. Every icon is 16x16 with a 1px dark outline, hard pixels (no anti-aliasing, alpha only fully on or off) and the same 32-colour palette as our [Pixel Dungeon & Overworld Tileset](https://larchrow.itch.io/larchrow-pixel-tileset) and Village & Cave Expansion, so items, map and UI look like one game. The iron and gold ore icons are the same pixels as the ore tiles in the Village & Cave pack.

### Made with code, no AI
Every icon is drawn by our own Python code: small hand-written pixel templates, shape rules and top-left lighting. Several are colour variants of one shape (e.g. potions in 5 colours, gem staffs, rings), but no two icons are identical. The inventory screenshot is a mockup to show the icons in use; the UI frames are not part of this pack (see our Clean Game UI Kit).

### Checked automatically (qa-report.json in the download)
- Every icon 16x16, 4x sheet is an exact nearest-neighbour copy
- Only the 32 palette colours; no semi-transparent pixels
- Every icon has a closed 1px outline and is not empty
- No duplicates: every pair of icons differs by at least 10 pixels
- Sheet, single PNGs, index and Godot files match; all .tres load in Godot 4.3
