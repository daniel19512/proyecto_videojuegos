WHAT'S IN THIS ZIP

  FBX/       one .fbx per model, for Unity and Unreal.
  Textures/  mdvl_palette.png, the one texture every model uses.
  Contents.csv  every item: name, group, size, triangles.
  License.txt

HOW THE MODELS ARE MADE
- 1 unit = 1 metre. A person is 1.8 m tall.
- Every model uses one material and one 256 x 256 palette texture, the same one
  across the whole library, so they all share a single material in your game.
- Flat shaded: each face is one flat colour. Keep it that way on import.
- Each model's origin is where you place it: on the ground for most, at a grid
  corner for pieces that snap together, at the hand grip for tools and weapons,
  and where it fixes to a wall for things that hang.

IMPORTING
Godot 4: drag the glTF folder in. The texture comes with each model.
Unity: drag the FBX folder and Textures in. Make one material with
  mdvl_palette.png as its base map and give it to the models. On the texture,
  set Filter Mode to Point and Compression to None. On the models, set Normals
  to Calculate and Smoothing Angle to 0.
Unreal 5: import the FBX files and the texture. Make one material with the
  texture into Base Color, Roughness about 0.85, and set the texture's filter
  to Nearest.
On any engine: nearest (no smoothing) filtering keeps the colours crisp.

FIELDS AND CROPS
- The furrow tiles are 1 x 1 m with their corner at the origin: lay them
  on a 1 m grid. furrow_01 and furrow_03 join along x with no seam.
  furrow_02 is a field's end: its ridges come in at x=0 and die out where
  the plough turned.
- Each tile has three ridges along x, at y = 1/6, 1/2 and 5/6 of a metre.
  A crop's origin is at the soil and its roots go 4 cm below, so stand it
  on a ridge with its origin at z = 0.055. furrow_03's four planting holes
  are at x = 0.28 and 0.72 on its two outer ridges.
- The garden bed is 2 x 1 m, its corner at the origin, on the same grid.
  Its two ridges are at y = 0.27 and 0.73; stand crops at z = 0.305.
- Wheat, corn and cabbage come in three stages: _01 just up, _02 growing,
  _03 ready. Swap one for the next as it grows.

THE MILLS
- Windmill: put windmill_sails_01's origin at (0, -2.96, 4.96) from
  windmill_body_01's origin, turned the same way, and the hub sits on the
  end of the windshaft.
- Watermill: put watermill_wheel_01's origin at (-1.75, 0.41, 2.20) from
  watermill_body_01's origin, turned the same way, and the wheel sits on
  its axle in the race. There is no water in the file: put your game's
  water at z = 0.55 in the race. To stand the mill in a river, sink it
  until that height is the river's water.
- The race is on the mill's -x side. To have it on the other side, mirror
  the mill (scale x by -1) and put the wheel at (1.75, 0.41, 2.20); the
  wheel itself needs no mirroring.
- Donkey mill: the origin is at the foot of its post. No animal comes with
  it; hitch your own to the crossbar at the end of the beam.
